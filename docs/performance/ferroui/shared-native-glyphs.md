# Designs 06/07: cross-layout native glyph geometry and blob reuse

`GlyphRunImpl` leases immutable `SharedGlyphRunData`. Repeated short glyph sequences reuse indices, positioned arrays, relative native bounds and font-state-specific Skia blobs across recreated layouts. Baseline origin remains per wrapper and translates relative bounds independently. Paragraph layout, trimming and hit testing remain in the formatter; native reuse alone does not skip paragraph formatting.

Keys include backend typeface identity, exact size and every glyph record. Hashes only locate candidates; full records are compared on a hit. Features, spacing, direction and culture are reflected in the shaped records. Custom list implementations and long runs bypass memoization. The LRU is bounded to 128 entries, 128 glyphs per entry and a conservative 2 MiB geometry/blob allowance; this is not an exact accounting of Skia's internal heap. Two-touch admission and per-typeface probe backoff avoid repeatedly investing in one-off native runs.

Every live wrapper and cache entry owns one reference. Eviction releases only the cache lease. Shared data keeps at most 18 effective edging/hinting/baseline font-state blobs and never evicts one while leased, since another wrapper may be drawing it. The first blob is inline; an 18-slot vector is allocated only for a second state. Wrapper-local caches borrow these blobs without owning disposal, preserving cheap warmed lookup. Shared blob creation is serialized and font disposal removes its cache entries. Input arrays are copied as needed, never retained as mutable glyph buffers.

`SharedGlyphRunTests` verifies sharing, separate baseline/bounds, exact PNG equality against uncached custom-list geometry in three edging modes, mutation isolation, option separation and live-run survival of eviction/disposal. `SharedGlyphBlobLifetimeTests` holds a borrowed blob while other wrappers cycle font states and read concurrently. `ColdGlyphCacheTests` verifies inline storage, expansion safety and probe recovery. Native benchmarks include both recreated identical glyphs and unique glyphs; the latter exposed the regression documented in [cold-native-runs.md](cold-native-runs.md).

These tests supplement actual browser pixel/input validation. They do not certify every physical GPU/backend or promise that cache synchronization is free on unique workloads.
